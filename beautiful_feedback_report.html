Strategy for Q2 Improvement
Based on the 503 student voices, we have identified primary areas for the next academic quarter.
priority #1
Boosting Classroom Engagement
With an average score of 3.93, engagement is our lowest dimension. We will focus on interactive learning tools to push this metric past the 4.15 target.
priority #2
Standardizing Doubt Resolution
While overall approachability is high, consistency varies across batches. Peer review sessions will help standardize the doubt-solving experience for all students.
